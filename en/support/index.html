FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Everything is stored on your device. Memomory has no accounts and no cloud server, so your memos never leave your phone.

What happens if I switch phones or delete the app?
Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes all of your memos. When you switch phones, your memos move with a full device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). A separate export and backup feature is planned for a future version.

How do keyword rules work?
Create rules in Settings › Keyword Rules, giving each keyword a category and tags. When you start a new memo, type the keyword first, then a space and the rest, for example "cake Tiramisu". After you send it, the memo is filed into Cooking › Baking › Desserts and tagged Recipe.

I deleted a memo by mistake. Can I get it back?
Deleted memos go to the Trash, where you can restore them anytime within 30 days. After 30 days they are removed automatically.

How much does Memomory cost?
All core features are free. In an age of information overload, we hope to offer a lightweight tool that makes things easy to find again, and helps anyone carry less of life's clutter. When you reach the limit on categories, tags, keyword rules, charts or the like, you can raise that one limit with a one-time purchase. Buy once and keep it forever, with no subscription. On a new device with the same Apple ID, tap Restore Purchases in Settings › Purchase. Prices are shown in the App Store.
