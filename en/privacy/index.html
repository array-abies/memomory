Memomory Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-27

In one sentence

Memomory does not collect any of your data. Your memos, categories, tags and settings are stored only on your device.

What is stored, and where

  • Everything you create in Memomory (memos, categories, tags, keyword rules, filters, chart settings and personal preferences) is stored on your device.
  • None of it is sent to a Memomory server, because there is no Memomory server.

What we don't do

  • No accounts: there is nothing to sign up for, and we don't know who you are.
  • No personal data collection: no name, email, location, contacts or any other identifying information.
  • No analytics or tracking: no statistics, advertising or behavioral analytics tools.
  • No third-party SDKs: the app contains no third-party services.
  • No ads.

Link titles

When you add or view a web address in Memomory (including in the sheet that opens when you share from another app), Memomory connects directly to that website to fetch the page title and icon, so the link is easier to recognize. Once it has them, it does not connect again.
The connection goes straight from your device to that website, not through us; we have no server. The website sees the ordinary connection details it would see if you opened it in a browser, such as your IP address. The title and icon are stored only on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases (higher limits and tips) are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Your payment details stay with Apple and never reach us. Memomory only asks the App Store, on your device, which items this Apple ID has bought, so it can unlock the matching limits.

Face ID lock

When you turn on the Face ID, Touch ID or passcode lock, iOS does the check. Memomory only learns whether it passed. It never receives or stores your face data, fingerprint or passcode.

Crash diagnostics

If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous diagnostic reports when the app runs into an error. We use them only to fix problems. Apple manages this sharing, it is off by default, and you can change it anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting and moving your data

  • Deleting the Memomory app deletes all of its data.
  • Your data is included in your device's full backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) and moves with it. Memomory does not upload anything on its own.

Children's privacy

Memomory does not collect data from anyone, so it does not collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future feature (for example, a backup option) means this policy needs to change, we will update this page and note the new effective date.
